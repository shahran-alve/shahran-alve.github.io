AI agents are starting to read our email, write our code, and act on our behalf. I work on making sure they can't be turned against us.

I'm a Ph.D. student at UT Dallas working on trustworthy machine learning and large language models, with a focus on agentic AI security. I study why LLM agents fail under prompt injection and trace each failure to its root cause, build multi-agent LLM systems that find and fix security vulnerabilities in code, expose hidden attacks on the data used to train LLMs, and design analytics systems that stop private records from leaking. My research appears in top-tier IEEE conferences and high-impact Q1 journals, in collaboration with NIST, TraCR, and King Saud University.

Research

Agentic AI security

Why guardrails fail under prompt injection, how to trace a failure to its root cause, and how multi-agent LLM systems can audit and repair code.

J. Cybersecurity and Privacy · ACM CODASPY 2027 & IEEE Access (under review)

Privacy-preserving analytics

Sequence-aware inference control: showing when query sequences leak protected records, and auditors that block them.

IEEE BigData 2026

Data integrity & evaluation

Integrity attacks on LLM pretraining pipelines, and statistically valid inference for ML on large longitudinal data.

IEEE BigData 2026

Explainable AI for healthcare

Deep learning with interpretable explanations (LIME, XAI, LLMs) for diagnosing dengue, COPD, osteoporosis, skin disease, and oral cancer.

Array · IEEE QPAIN · IEEE ICCIT · Global ENT 2027 talk

Network & IoT security

Lightweight ensemble and machine learning models for multi-class intrusion detection in IoT and software-defined networks.
